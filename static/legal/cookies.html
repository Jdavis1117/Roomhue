Cookie Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

RoomRoller uses as little browser storage as it can. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and nothing that tracks you across other websites.

Browser storage stays on your device. It is not sent to us.

Google sign-in

Google's sign-in service is loaded only when you choose to sign in and agree to continue. Once loaded, Google may set its own cookies (for example g_state, and cookies on google.com) to run sign-in securely. Those cookies are controlled by Google; see how Google uses cookies. If you never sign in, RoomRoller never loads anything from Google.

Your choices

  • Essential storage is needed for sign-in and to remember your choice, so it can't be turned off in the app.
  • Preferences are optional. Choose Cookie settings in the app to turn them on or off. Turning them off also clears what was saved. Favorites still work for the visit, and are saved to your account if you sign in.
  • Signing out removes the sign-in cookie. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time.
