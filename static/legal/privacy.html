Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

RoomRoller lets you preview paint colors on a photo of a room. It is run by Jordan Davis ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information RoomRoller handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: privacy@roomroller.com.

The short version

  • You can use RoomRoller without an account. Photos you open are used to find the walls and are not kept unless you save a room.
  • If you sign in with Google, we keep your Google account ID and first name. We do not keep your email address or profile photo.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your information.
  • You can delete your account and everything saved with it at any time, instantly.

What we collect and why

Photos you open

When you open a photo, it is sent to our server so it can find the walls, ceiling, and floor. That analysis runs entirely on our server; photos are not sent to any outside AI service. We use only the image itself: location, camera, and other embedded details are not kept. Large phone photos are reduced in size in your browser before they are sent. An opened photo is held in the server's memory while you edit and is removed after two hours without use, or sooner. It is not written to storage unless you save the room.

Rooms you save

If you are signed in and choose Save, we store the photo (re-encoded without embedded details), a small preview image, the outlines of each wall, the colors and finishes you chose, the room's name, and when you saved it. Saved rooms are linked only to your account ID and are visible only to you.

Favorite colors

If you mark colors as favorites while signed in, we store that list (each color's brand, name, number, and value) with your account so it's there on any device. If you aren't signed in, favorites stay on your device, and only if you allow preferences; when you sign in, they're added to your account.

Links you share

If you choose Share, we store a copy of that room's photo, the painted version, the room's name, and the colors used, and give you a link. Anyone who has the link can see them, without signing in. Shared pages tell search engines not to list them. Each link is tied to your account; you can delete it anytime from Collection, and deleting your account deletes all your links.

Your Google account

Sign-in is optional and only needed to save rooms. When you sign in, Google sends us a signed token confirming who you are. We check it and keep only your Google account ID (a number) and your first name, which is shown in the menu. We do not receive your password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else in your Google account.

Your agreement to these terms

When you sign in, we record which version of the Terms of Service and this policy you agreed to, and when.

Cookies and device storage

We use one cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser's storage for your cookie choice and, if you allow it, your recent colors and layout. Google's sign-in service is loaded only when you choose to sign in. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

Technical logs

Our hosting provider records basic request information, such as IP address, time, and the page requested, to run and protect the service. We do not use it to track you.

What we don't do

  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. The only things made public are links you choose to share.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models or for any purpose other than showing you your rooms.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process photos, saved rooms, and your account details to provide the service you ask for (performance of a contract). We load Google's sign-in service and keep optional preferences with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We keep technical logs for security, which is in our legitimate interest.

How long we keep information

  • Photos you open but don't save: up to two hours in server memory.
  • Saved rooms, favorite colors, and shared links: until you remove them or delete your account.
  • Your account ID, first name, and agreement record: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in cookie: 60 days, or until you sign out.
  • Technical logs: for the period our hosting provider keeps them.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account, every room saved with it, and your favorite colors at any time. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. Saved rooms are removed from storage right away.

To delete, sign in on RoomRoller, then come back to this page, or open the menu and choose Delete account and data. You can also email privacy@roomroller.com from any address. We'll confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download, or delete your information, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, email privacy@roomroller.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose other than providing the service.

Children

RoomRoller is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we'll delete the account.

Security

RoomRoller is served over HTTPS. The sign-in cookie is signed so it can't be altered, and saved rooms can only be reached with the account that saved them. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't save photos you wouldn't want stored online.

Where information is processed

RoomRoller is operated from the United States, and information is stored and processed there.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll ask you to agree again the next time you sign in.
